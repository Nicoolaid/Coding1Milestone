The first camera phone is believed to be Japan’s Kyocera VP-210 launched in May 1999. Over the years, many models have claimed to be the first phone with a camera. Prototypes and experimental models consisting of a device with a built-in camera have existed since 1993. However, many believe it is safe to say that the Kyocera VP-210 is officially the first phone with a mobile camera sold commercially to the public. With a 0.11MP camera and a storage capacity of 20 pictures, this phone became a hit. It even came with a stand for users to take photos of themselves efficiently. Today, cameras have become a default feature for any modern smartphone or mobile device, and we can thank the Kyocera VP-210 for that.
